# Minefy

**Mine production planning software for modern mining teams.**

Minefy helps mining operations bring planning, scheduling, production updates, reporting, and AI-assisted planning support into one practical workspace.

Built in Finland, Minefy is designed for the pace and detail of real mine planning work: changing schedules, dependencies, resources, locations, progress updates, and decisions that need clear operational context.

## What We Do

Minefy is built for mining operations, contractors, and planning teams that need a clearer way to coordinate production from plan to execution.

Our software combines:

- **Production planning** for short-term and operational mine schedules
- **Visual scheduling** for tasks, dependencies, resources, shifts, and progress
- **Mine maps** for connecting plans to real locations
- **Mobile production updates** for keeping planning data close to the operation
- **Reporting** for clearer production visibility
- **Minefy AI** for scheduling context, resource planning, task dependencies, and day-to-day planning support

## Why Minefy

Mine planning software should be powerful without feeling heavy.

Minefy is being built to be faster to adopt, easier to understand, and closer to the practical rhythm of production planning, while still respecting the operational detail that mining teams rely on.
